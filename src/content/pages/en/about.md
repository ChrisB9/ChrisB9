I am Chris, {age} years old, a senior software engineer and freelancer as well as a digital nomad, with an emphasis on PHP backend development.

Alongside the work I do for my clients, I am involved in the open source community: writing translations, fixing bugs, building features, and working on private projects of my own.

A few years ago I began to question how I work, and realised how much travelling the world means to me. That is how I became a digital nomad, and I currently live in Japan, in Kanagawa Prefecture, and mostly work from here.

This lifestyle brings a lot of travelling with it, meeting new people, new opportunities and cultures which has become my main hobby. It also means that much of my free time happens outdoors.

I love hiking, paragliding, simply being in the mountains or by the sea, and picking up new sports such as surfing. I also play handball in the local community.
